# Team brief — <debate id>

Written for the team, in the human's language. One line first.

**Conclusion:** <one line>

1. **What was being decided, researched or tested**, and what was not.
2. **The criteria** and their weights (or the angles, for research).
3. **The options** considered (or the landscape).
4. **The weighty reasons** for the result (or what is established).
5. **The defense:** the strongest case for the losing side, and why it lost
   (or what is contested, and the crux).
6. **The result** (decision IDs, the map, the verdict) and what waits for the human.
7. **Minorities:** who disagreed and what would change their mind.
8. **Next steps** and the gates that could reverse the decision.
9. **Why it can be trusted:** rounds, independent and fresh panels, model
   families, lead checks, cross-review.

## Audio-script outline (optional)

Same order, spoken: a 20-second summary, then sections 1–8 at about one
minute each, closing on next steps.
